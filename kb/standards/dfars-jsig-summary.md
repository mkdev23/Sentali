# DFARS and JSIG high-level summary for builders

## DFARS essentials
- **Scope:** DoD contractors handling CDI/CUI.
- **Key clause:** 252.204-7012 (safeguarding + 72-hr incident reporting).
- **Control basis:** Implement NIST SP 800-171 controls; maintain SSP and POA&M.
- **CMMC linkage:** Formalizes assessment/certification expectations over time.

## JSIG snapshot
- **Context:** Guidance for securing IT supporting Special Access Programs.
- **Approach:** Builds on NIST RMF controls with SAP-specific overlays and approvals.
- **Builder focus:** Strong access controls, auditing, enclave isolation, AC/SC/AU rigor.

## Practical guidance
- **Cloud use:** FedRAMP Moderate+ for CUI; log retention; incident response playbooks.
- **Documentation:** Keep evidence (configs, policies, diagrams) traceable to controls.

- DFARS 252.204-7012, NIST SP 800-171, DoD JSIG
